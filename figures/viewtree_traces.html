ViewTree Traces

Six real reasoning traces from the trained ViewTree system (Qwen2.5-VL-7B + Stage III fusion adapter + calibrated confidence head) answering VSI-Bench questions. Each question runs the same loop: sample video evidence, reconstruct the room with VGGT, branch into five rendered viewpoints scored by the confidence head, prune to the top two, then decide — stop on consensus, or arbitrate between the fused multi-view answer and the frames-only direct answer. Three successes, three instructive failures.
